BizDash

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

BizDash is a sales and expense tracker for small businesses, made by BraxCode Digitals Foundation in Mwanza, Tanzania ("we", "us"). This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect

  • From your Google account when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • What you enter in BizDash: your business name and type, products and stock, sales, expenses, customer records you add, and your currency.
  • Subscription records: your plan, the date and amount of a payment we confirm, and when Premium starts and ends. Payments are made by mobile money outside the app. We never see your mobile money PIN or card details.
  • Notifications we send you inside the app, such as payment confirmations and plan reminders.
  • On your device: your browser keeps a sign-in session and a few small settings so the app works. We also set one small cookie named bizdash_member on braxcode.com. It only tells our website to show "Log in" instead of "Get Started", and it contains no personal information.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers may record basic details such as IP address and browser type for security and to keep the service running.

2. How we use it

  • To run BizDash and show you your own records.
  • To confirm payments and activate or renew Premium.
  • To send you reminders and notices about your plan.
  • To give you support when you contact us.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

3. Who can see your data

Your business records are visible to you. We, as the operators of BizDash, can access the data when needed to give support or confirm a payment. We use these service providers to run the app: Google (sign-in), Supabase (database and authentication) and Vercel (hosting). We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access rules make sure each account can only read its own business data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account protected.

5. How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep your information while your account is active. To delete your account and business data, open Settings → Delete my account in the app, which prepares a WhatsApp message to us, or message us on WhatsApp directly. We delete your account and business data within 7 days. We may keep basic payment records if we need them for accounting.

6. Your choices

  • Correct your name and business details any time in Settings.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to delete your data (see section 5).

7. Children

BizDash is for business owners and is not meant for people under 18.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
