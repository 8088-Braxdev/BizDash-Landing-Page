Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We also store the business information you enter yourself — business name, type, products, sales, expenses, and customer records you add.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run the app for you: showing your dashboard, tracking your inventory, and calculating your reports. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third parties.

Payments

BizDash does not store any card or bank details. Payments are currently confirmed manually — you notify us directly, and we verify and activate your plan. We never ask for your mobile money PIN or card number.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database provider. Access to your data is restricted using row-level security, meaning only you (and BizDash administrators for support purposes) can view your business data.

Your Rights

You can request to delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. We will remove your information within a reasonable timeframe.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the Help Center in the app, or contact BraxCode Digitals Foundation directly.
